Klipsch

Klipsch in-wall and in-ceiling.

The CDT ceilings and the R-5502-W II in-wall, all with the Tractrix horn. Pairs the Reference and Reference Premiere box families with hidden surrounds and heights, or builds a whole hidden theater. Not at Best Buy: R-2650-C II, R-5650-W II, R-5800-W II, CS-16C II, and the CDT-2650-C II is gone.

Polk Audio

Polk Vanishing Series.

Thin-bezel, paintable in-walls and in-ceilings. The RC80i / RC60i / RC85i rounds that used to be the value pick are gone from Best Buy and the V-series never came, so the RT line is what you quote.

KEF

KEF Ci Series.

Uni-Q in the wall and ceiling: the Q-badged Ci models match the Q Series, the THX reference models match R Series. Deep bench at Best Buy, all sold direct.

MartinLogan

MartinLogan Motion CI, Motion XT CI and Installer Series.

Motion CI and XT CI carry the Folded Motion tweeter from the box speakers. Installer Series is the budget line; Helos are angled ceilings; Icon and Tribute are the in-wall flagships. The angled XTC8-HT is gone from Best Buy.

Definitive Technology

Definitive Technology Dymension CI and DI.

Dymension CI is the current line that matches the DM box speakers: PRO is the standard driver set, MAX the step up. The older DI rounds are still sold direct.

Bowers & Wilkins

Bowers & Wilkins CI300, CI600, CI700 S2 and CI800.

CI300 and CI600 are sold in pairs, CI700 S2 and CI800 singly. In the CI600 numbering the lower last digit is the better speaker (CCM662 above CCM663 above CCM664 above CCM665).

JBL

JBL Stage architectural.

Three lines, all sold by Best Buy: Stage architectural (value, aluminum dome in an acoustic-lens waveguide, in-wall and in-ceiling), Studio 6 in-walls (premium, the compression tweeter from JBL's Synthesis cinema line) and the Conceal invisible in-wall.

Sonance

Sonance, line by line.

Best Buy lists about 270 Sonance items; most are finish and quantity variants, brackets and enclosures. Mag Series prices move with promos.

When Sonance is the right call, and when to step up

Sell itHeights and surrounds in any build. Whole-home background audio. A ceiling-only room where nothing can show. A $500 budget that still wants 5.1 (MAG5.1R). Outdoor and landscape jobs (Mariner, Mag outdoor, Sonarray).
Step upFront left, center and right for a real media room. In-ceiling speakers fire down at the floor; dialog and imaging come from the screen wall. Put the money in in-wall LCRs (VP62 LCR, VX62 LCR) or a box front stage, and keep Sonance for the ceiling.
The honest Mag pitchMAG6R at $149 a pair is a good 6.5 in ceiling speaker at a price nobody else touches. It is not a $749 speaker; the comp value is the story, the sound is "fine for ceilings." Say that and you keep the customer's trust for the upsell.
What is actually differentMag has a magnetic flush grille and a round bezel-free look. VX and VP are the installer lines: better drivers, pivoting tweeters, LCR versions, thin-line and single-stereo options. Invisible is plastered over. Impact subs are wireless-ready.

Yamaha

Yamaha, what is left.

Best Buy has let the Yamaha speaker line go to marketplace sellers. Two pieces are still sold by Best Buy itself: the NS-IC600 in-ceiling, on clearance, and the NS-AW294 outdoor pair.

Sonos

Sonos Architectural by Sonance.

Passive speakers built by Sonance and tuned by Sonos Amp with Trueplay. Every pair needs an Amp, so quote them together. Best Buy sells the two in-ceilings and the outdoor pair; the in-wall pair is marketplace only now.

Russound

Russound in-ceiling and Acclaim.

Ceilings sold as singles. Whole-home audio pieces that pair with Russound or Sonance amplifiers.

Bose

Bose, the one passive line.

Everything else Bose sells at Best Buy is wireless (Lifestyle Ultra, Surround Speakers, soundbars), so this is the only Bose row.

WiiM · sold and shipped by Best Buy

WiiM streaming amps and the WiiM soundbar.

The receiver alternative for a music-first stereo or 2.1 room: a small amp with every streaming service in the app, HDMI ARC from the TV, a sub out and room correction. No surround and no path to a center or rears, so it is for the customer who will never grow into 5.1. In the Family builder, a 2-channel build shows a "WiiM amp" chip under the receiver level. Prices are what bestbuy.com showed on Nov 15, 2026.
