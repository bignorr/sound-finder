When Sonance is the right call, and when to step up

Sell itHeights and surrounds in any build. Whole-home background audio. A ceiling-only room where nothing can show. A $500 budget that still wants 5.1 (MAG5.1R). Outdoor and landscape jobs (Mariner, Mag outdoor, Sonarray).
Step upFront left, center and right for a real media room. In-ceiling speakers fire down at the floor; dialog and imaging come from the screen wall. Put the money in in-wall LCRs (VP62 LCR, VX62 LCR) or a box front stage, and keep Sonance for the ceiling.
The honest Mag pitchMAG6R at $149 a pair is a good 6.5 in ceiling speaker at a price nobody else touches. It is not a $749 speaker; the comp value is the story, the sound is "fine for ceilings." Say that and you keep the customer's trust for the upsell.
What is actually differentMag has a magnetic flush grille and a round bezel-free look. VX and VP are the installer lines: better drivers, pivoting tweeters, LCR versions, thin-line and single-stereo options. Invisible is plastered over. Impact subs are wireless-ready.
